## Problem statement
What keeps happening, how often, and what it costs (orders delayed, hours spent).

## Root cause analysis
Five whys, or a timeline of the failing path through the code. End with the
root cause in one sentence.

## Workaround (known error)
The exact, tested steps that restore service until the fix ships. Link the
standard change if the workaround touches production data.

## Permanent fix
What changes, where (file, config, process), and the change record that
delivers it. How will you prove it works (test, drill)?

## Closure
Fix deployed on YYYY-MM-DD; no repeat incidents since. Knowledge article updated.
